● docs/database.md is written. It has the critique, the Mermaid ER diagram, the
  traceability tables for sections 1, 4, 5, 6 and 8, the constraints that can't
  be drawn, the seed checksums, the trade-offs and the open questions. It
  contains no SQL. pnpm format, lint and type-check pass. pnpm test found no
  test files.

  Where the draft would fail against the brief
  1. Payment review fields: one reviewed_by/at/reason set means a void
     overwrites the approver. I split them into reviewed_* + reject_reason and
     voided_* + void_reason.
  2. Payment rules were missing: nothing tied unapplied_sen, method, slip_path,
     gateway_bill_id and recorded_by together. CHECKs now do. slip_path must sit
     under the payment's own <org_id>/ folder and point to an existing Storage
     object.
  3. Cross-lease allocation: an allocation could link a payment on lease A to an
     invoice on lease B. Composite FKs (payment_id, lease_id) and (invoice_id, 
     lease_id) make that impossible.
  4. Over-allocation: nothing stopped it. I added deferred constraint triggers
     as a backstop behind the lease row lock.
  5. Invoice shape: nothing stopped a deposit with a period, a rent invoice
     without one, a duplicate period or two deposits. I added CHECKs (including
     due_date = period_start for rent) and partial unique indexes.
  6. gateway_bills: paid wasn't terminal, and UNIQUE(gateway_bill_id) alone
     doesn't stop a payment pointing at another lease's bill or a different
     amount. I added a bill state-machine trigger and a composite FK
     (gateway_bill_id, lease_id, amount_sen).
  7. audit_log: a REVOKE doesn't bind the owner or service role, and
     session_replication_role = replica bypasses ordinary triggers. The fix is
     raise-triggers on UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE, set to ENABLE ALWAYS.
  8. Collections report: grouping by UTC puts the seeded RM 2,000 (2026-03-01
     00:30 MYT) in February. The design uses +08 month bounds and a partial
     index.

  What the draft got right and stays: no stored paid or status,
  memberships.user_id as PK, composite (x_id, org_id) FKs, the exclusion
  constraint, bigint sen, RESTRICT.

  Seed numbers: I derived them by hand and all match your figures.
  - Invoice counts: Aisyah 7, Ben 4, Probe 13, Chen 13, total 37.
  - Last periods: Aisyah's is 77419 (150000 × 16/31 = 77419.35). Ben's is 50015
    (100029 × 15/30 = 50014.5, rounded half up).
  - No proration: Probe's and Chen's last periods are full. Their end dates are
    the day before the next anchor, so neither is prorated.
  - Collections report: Northwind for 2026-01..03 is 300000, 0, 200000.

  Deviations from your docs:
  - Void deletes allocation rows, as the brief says. That contradicts AGENTS
    "never hard-delete financial rows", so it needs a superseding ADR. The
    design snapshots the allocations in audit_log.details.
  - There is no paid_cents column, because the brief says paid is the sum of
    allocations.

  Open questions: there are 15 in section 8, each with a recommendation. These
  need your call:

  - Q8: require transaction_status = completed before settling a bill. That
    departs from the brief's table, which decides on paid and paid_amount only.
  - Q10: allow several pending bills per lease (my recommendation).

  Where I deviated from your instructions

  - authenticated keeps EXECUTE on the 5 helper functions and USAGE on schema 
    private. Postgres checks both when a policy calls a function, so revoking

     - paid_at accepts the brief format (2026-04-01 00:30:12 +0800) or ISO 8601
       with an offset. It must be a real calendar date, otherwise 422. There is
       no fallback to now.
     - Transaction:
       - The lease row is locked first.
       - updateMany ... WHERE id AND status <> 'paid' runs next.

       - A count of 1 allocates, inserts one approved online payment with the

       recorded_by and reviewed_by are null, and gateway_bill_id is set. The

       overlap helper.
